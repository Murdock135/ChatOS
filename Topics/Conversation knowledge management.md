# Conversation knowledge management

Conversations should accumulate into a navigable body of knowledge, with related discussions connected across apps and time.

## Explicit direction

- The assistant is Zayan's main Chief of Staff.
- All chats are intended to enter this Obsidian vault and be clustered by topic or semantic meaning.
- Source scope includes Codex, ChatGPT, and other chat apps.
- The vault should also contain skills, memory, and accessible files related to the assistant's operating behavior. See [[System/Index|Chief of Staff system]].

## Supporting conversations

- [[2026-10-22 Chief of Staff and chat archive]]

## Current implementation

The vault has a chronological conversation folder and linked topic notes. These links are an initial meaning-based organization, not a computed embedding cluster.

The system area contains vault-owned operating guidance and working memory, plus a verified dated archive of accessible Codex behavior files. System snapshots refresh manually.

## Unresolved

- Which other apps should be included?
- What import and ongoing capture mechanisms are available for each app?
