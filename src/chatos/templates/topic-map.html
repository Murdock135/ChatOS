Method and limits

Existing main_subject and subtopic subject fields are embedded separately with local MiniLM. Titles, operations, and summary prose are available for inspection but are excluded from embeddings. These are labels from the older prompt, without fresh compact-label generation.

Each model has its own UMAP layout (cosine, 15 neighbors, minimum distance 0.15, seed 42). Switching models changes the coordinate system. Filters retain existing positions. The five related points come from other conversations using original embedding distances, rather than screen distance.
